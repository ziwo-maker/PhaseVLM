Idea Discovery Report: PhaseVLM Stage 3 Replacement

Direction: Replace the invalidated OOD/selection-centered Stage-3 story with a falsifiable control mechanism for policy-responsive counterfactual distillation.

Date: 2026-08-18 Pipeline: research-lit -> idea-creator -> novelty-check -> research-review -> research-refine-pipeline Assurance: same-family critical review is complete but provisional; the Semantic Scholar search became rate-limited (HTTP 429), so a final pre- submission search remains required.

Executive Summary

The previous innovation claim should be replaced. OOD acquisition, diversity selection, frozen-neighbor long-horizon labels, and cross-horizon reliability gating have all failed in closed-loop evaluation. The strongest reproducible mechanism is that a counterfactual teacher must let non-target intersections respond with the deployed policy, otherwise long-horizon labels artificially favor through phases and cause coordination collapse.

The recommended replacement is Policy-Responsive Counterfactual Teacher Construction (PRCT): force a candidate phase at one intersection while all other intersections re-plan with the deployed source policy every 30 seconds over a 120-second CityFlow rollout. This replaces the invalid frozen-neighbor teacher and directly explains the observed action-collapse failure. PRCAT (advantage-weighted conservative distillation) is retained only as a conditional robustness module; its online benefit has not yet been established.

Structural Gap

No retrieved work combined all of: (1) a frozen VLM traffic policy as the multi-intersection rollout policy, (2) a forced one-intersection candidate action, (3) neighbor re-planning during the counterfactual horizon, and (4) a source-relative trust rule that prevents low-value counterfactual labels from moving a strong deployed policy. The novelty is not advantage weighting; it is the closed-loop counterfactual evaluator and the measured coordination bias it corrects.

Ranked Ideas

Idea 1: Policy-Responsive Counterfactual Teacher Construction (PRCT) - RECOMMENDED

Method:

  1. From each source-policy state, force one candidate phase at one intersection.
  2. Every other intersection executes the same source policy again at each 30-second decision point during a 120-second CityFlow rollout.
  3. Use all four resulting global costs to produce the target distribution and compare frozen versus policy-responsive teachers across horizons and label budgets.

Hypothesis: frozen-neighbor labels encode a coordination-inconsistent counterfactual. Re-planning non-target intersections with the source policy removes that bias, preserves phase diversity, and improves closed-loop traffic control when target-flow headroom exists.

Existing evidence: responsive H120 avoided the frozen long-horizon through-action collapse; it improved older Jinan-to-Hangzhou results and Jinan-real in-network AWT by 6.8% over nine seeds. Its held-out Jinan-2500 gain was indistinguishable from zero, motivating the conservative component.

Novelty: Medium-high (7/10, provisional). The forced-local-action, policy-responsive multi-intersection CityFlow teacher and its measured frozen-neighbor coordination bias are the defensible delta; generic counterfactual credit assignment and policy fitting are prior art.

Risk: Medium. It is a simulator-specific empirical contribution and must replicate across the original cross-network setting and held-out demand shift.

Idea 2: PRCAT Conservative Distillation - CONDITIONAL MODULE

PRCAT weights the PRCT loss by source-relative counterfactual advantage and adds a source-action KL prior on low-advantage rows. Its valid K=118, training-seed-2024 pilot reached AWT=42.48 +/- 0.22 across three CityFlow evaluation seeds. This improves on zero-shot (43.98) but is 3.04s worse than the matched unweighted responsive-H120 training seed (39.44). Because AWR/A2PR/preference-style conservatism are prior art and the isolated pilot is negative against the teacher baseline, PRCAT is not a core contribution and is not being expanded to more training seeds.

Idea 3: Topology-Conditioned Prefix Hypernetwork - DEFERRED

Potentially high ceiling, but it needs six or more diverse training topologies and leave-one-topology-out tests. The current two-network evidence cannot support it; it is deferred rather than pursued with a weak evaluation.

Novelty Verification

The closest generic families are COMA, model-based/dual-policy imitation, advantage-weighted regression, A2PR, and behavior preference regression. PRCAT must not claim a new generic conservative offline RL objective. The defensible core delta is the policy-responsive, multi-intersection simulator oracle that evaluates a local phase deviation against the source policy's dynamic network response, together with causal evidence for the frozen-neighbor coordination bias. External Critical Review

A same-family adversarial reviewer judged the teacher mechanism as the only current paper-level contribution and rejected PRCAT as a proven replacement. Its minimum credible package is: Jinan-to-Hangzhou and Jinan-to-Jinan-2500 shifts; K=118 with nine training seeds plus K=32; zero-shot, frozen C30, unweighted responsive H120, PRCAT weight-only, KL-only, and full PRCAT arms; AWT primary with ATT/AQL, p95/max movement wait, spillback, and action entropy/frequencies as safety outcomes. It also requires evidence that high PRCAT advantage predicts later benefit rather than merely encoding a local min-max ranking. Therefore PRCAT is a conditional module, there is no policy improvement guarantee, and a one-training-seed result is exploratory only.
